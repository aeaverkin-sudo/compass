# Consent and acceptable use — draft

This is a working draft for the checkbox, `/terms`, and `/privacy`.
It is not the final legal text. A lawyer and the GDPR AI clause come later.
Version id in code: `1.0` (`TERMS_VERSION` and `PRIVACY_VERSION`, stored together as `CONSENT_VERSION`).

The checkbox is off until the person turns it on. Confirm and registration stay disabled until then.
The stored row is the version, the time, and the user id, on the trial path or the register path.

## Points the draft must cover

1. Compass uses AI.
2. A card may not be used for anything illegal. That includes pornography, weapons, drugs, resale of prescription medicine, sanctioned goods, and any other illegal use.
3. We store the data securely.
4. Do not put sensitive data on a card. A phone number or an Instagram handle is usually already public; sharing it here is voluntary.
5. We do not scan every card. We may check one suspicious word or item. We do not review the whole card as a routine.
6. We cooperate with the authorities when the law requires it.
7. We do not sell the data and we do not give it to third parties.
8. Sign-in is Google, or a starter code sent by email. That code is the password until the person changes it. The person is responsible for keeping the profile secure.
